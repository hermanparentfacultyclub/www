# Support Herman PFC

Every contribution helps the **Herman Parent Faculty Club (PFC)** support our students and strengthen the Herman school community.

Your donations help us provide student activities, family events, teacher and staff appreciation, campus improvements, and other programs and opportunities throughout the school year.

## Where Your Donation Goes

Your support helps Herman PFC fund and support programs such as:

* **Student activities and enrichment**
* **Family and community events**
* **Teacher and staff appreciation**
* **Campus improvements**
* **Special projects and school needs**
* **PFC-sponsored programs and activities**

As a smaller school community, every contribution has an impact. Together, our families can help provide experiences and resources that benefit Herman students throughout the year.
