# PFC Meetings

Our Parent Faculty Club (PFC) holds a monthly virtual community meeting for Herman Intermediate School and AdVENTURE STEM Patriots families. These sessions are a chance to hear updates, ask questions, and get involved with our school community.

**Format:** Virtual, via Google Meet
**Time:** 6:00 PM
**Join link:** Shared through ParentSquare on the day of each meeting

Dates are subject to change. Watch ParentSquare for reminders and that month's Google Meet link before each session.

## Get Involved

All Herman & AdVENTURE STEM families are welcome to join any PFC meeting — no RSVP required. See our [Get Involved]({{ '/get-involved/' | relative_url }}) page for other ways to support the PFC.
